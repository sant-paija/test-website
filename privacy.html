Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected and used when you interact with the Soccer Team Bot via WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the bot to RSVP for games, we may collect:

  • WhatsApp Display Name: To identify you in the team lists.
  • Phone Number: To manage your participation and contact you regarding game updates.
  • Game Preferences: Skill level or position (if provided) to help balance teams.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information is used solely for the purpose of organizing weekly soccer games, including:

  • Tracking RSVPs and attendance.
  • Generating balanced team rosters.
  • Sending notifications about game times or cancellations.

3. Data Sharing

Your name and RSVP status will be visible to other members of the WhatsApp group to facilitate game organization. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or external organizations.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as you are an active participant in our soccer sessions. You can request to have your data removed at any time.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct or delete your information.
  • Stop receiving messages by leaving the WhatsApp group or blocking the bot.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please contact the bot administrator directly via the WhatsApp group.

This bot is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta Platforms, Inc.
